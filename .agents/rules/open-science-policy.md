# Open Science & Transparency Policy (SE 2630397-4)

Everything in this repository is 100% **Open Science, Open Source, and Radically Transparent**.

## Rules for Reports and Documentation:
1. **NEVER use the word "CONFIDENTIAL" or mark any document, header, footer, PDF, script, or markdown as confidential.**
2. When generating PDF reports or documentation headers, always use open science and patent-pending headers, for example:
   - Header: `"INVERTED MAML ANALOG MATRIX — PATENT PENDING SE 2630397-4"`
   - Subtitle / Footer: `"Open Science Hardware | 100% Pure Physical Silicon Execution | Trion T120 SoC"`
3. Maintain full scientific and empirical transparency: publish all raw hardware telemetry, test scripts, and benchmark statistics openly.
