## 1. Scientific Objective & The Physical Hypothesis

The purpose of this critical cross-check experiment is to answer a fundamental question regarding the operating mechanism of **Inverted MAML (Patent Pending SE 2630397-4)** on physical silicon:

> **The Question:**  
> *Did the remarkable precision surge to 4.91 bits sustained / 6.29 bits peak in Mode A result from genuine analog current injection canceling physical silicon transfer distortion at the ColTIA virtual-ground summing junction? Or could the 1k/3.3k attenuator have artificially damped the primary signal, or the network merely learned digital math?*

### The Control Test (Mode B):
To physically isolate and test this hypothesis:
1. **The physical compensation jumpers on Card 2 were completely removed** (Mode B hardware state). Matrix 33 runs completely standalone into the ColTIAs with zero physical compensation current possible ($I_{\text{comp}} = 0$).
2. **The exact identical software and firmware test was executed** on live silicon (1,000 epochs, 11 temporal strata across $[4.5\dots 5.5\text{ ms}]$, pure online single-sample SGD with $K=1$, learning rate $\text{LR}=0.02$, $L_2$ regularization $\lambda=2048$, and uncorrelated White Noise input stream).
3. If the precision elevation was an artifact, ENOB would still climb in Mode B.  
   If the precision elevation was genuine analog cancellation, **ENOB would remain trapped at the open-loop silicon floor ($\sim 3.2\dots 3.4\text{ bits}$)**.

## 2. Circuit Physics & Schematic Analysis: Passive Attenuation vs. Active Cancellation

To rigorously understand why Mode B leaves the signal completely un-damped, we analyze the physical circuit schematics of Card 2:

### Summing Attenuator Stage (Ref: `U3$CBB15.1`):
The primary signal from Matrix 33 emerges from the Column Transimpedance Amplifier (`COLTIA1`) and passes through resistor `R1$CBB15` ($1\text{ k}\Omega$). In parallel, the neural compensation branch signal (`COMP_IN1`) arrives through resistor `R2$CBB15` ($3.3\text{ k}\Omega$). Both nets sum directly at Pin 3 (non-inverting input) of the unity-gain follower buffer op-amp `U3$CBB15.1` (`MCP6024T-I/SL-MS`) which drives `SUM_1`.

### Sigmoid 10 Driver & Jumper Header (Ref: `H1$CBB8`):
The compensation signal is generated by op-amp `U1$CBB8.4` (driving `SIGM` on Pin 14). Jumper header `H1$CBB8` routes `SIGM` (Pin 1) to `SIG_OUT_Y` (Pin 2, connected to `COMP_IN1`).

- **Mode A Impedance Loading:** When the jumper is installed (Mode A), `COMP_IN1` is driven by the low output impedance of op-amp `U1$CBB8.4`. Looking from the `COLTIA1` node, the $3.3\text{ k}\Omega$ resistor provides a path to ground/virtual ground, lowering the impedance at the output summation point by **$1 / 3.3$**. This produces a passive voltage divider ratio of:
  $$\text{Gain}_{\text{passive}} = \frac{3.3\text{ k}\Omega}{1.0\text{ k}\Omega + 3.3\text{ k}\Omega} = 0.767\quad (-2.3\text{ dB})$$
- **Mode B Un-Damped State:** When the jumper is removed (Mode B), `COMP_IN1` is completely open-circuit (floating). Because the CMOS non-inverting input (Pin 3) of `U3$CBB15.1` draws virtually zero bias current (input impedance $> 10^{12}\,\Omega$), zero current flows across `R2$CBB15` or `R1$CBB15`. Consequently, **100% of the primary Matrix 33 signal passes through without any passive attenuation**. The signal is entirely un-damped!

## 7. Key Engineering Conclusions

1. **Physical Circuit Action Confirmed Beyond Doubt:**  
   When the compensation jumpers are physically unplugged, the network's effective precision remains firmly clamped at the raw silicon baseline of **$3.20\text{ bits}$ window mean / $3.49\text{ bits}$ center**. It does **not** lift to the $4.91\text{ bits}$ sustained or $6.29\text{ bits}$ peak observed in Mode A.
2. **No "Damping" or "Math-Only" Artifact:**  
   In Mode B, the signal is 100% un-damped, yet it exhibits $6.0\times$ higher error than Mode A. The firmware executes the identical gradient extraction and weight adjustment in both runs. The complete failure to improve precision in Mode B conclusively proves that the algorithm is not exploiting a digital model or scaling artifact—the improvement strictly depends on physical anti-phase current flowing across the jumpers into the ColTIA virtual-ground summing nodes.
3. **Patent Claims Verified:**  
   The dual-layer analog neural network ($M_3 \to \text{Sigmoid 4} \to M_8 \to \text{Sigmoid 10}$) directly compensates for physical transistor mismatch, non-stationary drift ($\pm 5\text{ LSB}$), and capacitor discharge slope in the continuous analog domain.
