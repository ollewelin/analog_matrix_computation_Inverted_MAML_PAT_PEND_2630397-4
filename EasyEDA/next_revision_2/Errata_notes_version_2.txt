3. Key Errata Fixes & Component Changes from PCB v1.3.2 -> v2.0:
   - Item 1 (Op-Amp Upgrade for DAC2 In-Data Rows):
     U1$CBB16 and U2$CBB16 are upgraded to MCP6444-E/SL (SOIC-14, LCSC: C108882).
     High drive capability eliminates dynamic row voltage sag during simultaneous multi-column load.
     Weight DAC buffers remain MCP6024T-I/SL-MS (LCSC: C54539958, Qty 30).
   - Item 2 (Summing Junction Attenuator Optimization):
     Summing junction resistors R2$CBB15, R3$CBB15, R4$CBB15, R6$CBB15, R7$CBB15, R8$CBB15
     updated from 3.9 kOhm to 5.1 kOhm (0402WGF5101TCE, LCSC: C25905, Qty 6).
     Provides optimal Vernier authority with robust Hadamard SNR (+18 dB, 40..60 LSB swing).
   - Item 3 (JTAG & CSI Mode Pin Pull-Ups):
     RDX2 / CSI pin pull-up resistor updated to 10 kOhm to ensure clean single-device
     JTAG boundary-scan mode on T120 Master and T20 Controller.
   - Item 4 (LED & Control Resistors):
     R2$CBB22 updated to 160 Ohm (0603WAF1600T5E, LCSC: C22814, Qty 1).
   - Item 5 (Ethernet PHY Magnetics):
     Center tap capacitor 10 nF to GND for LAN8720A / HR911105A.
   - Item 6 (Reset Net):
     Added dedicated T120_CRESET_N net.
